FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

What is MIYORI?

MIYORI is a luxury native Android music player crafted around high-bitrate streaming, synchronized lyrics, local music ownership, and self-hosted Navidrome integration. Built in 100% native Kotlin under 11 MB.

How does local ownership work?

Downloaded audio is saved as standard audio files in your device storage at Music/Miyori with ID3v2.4 metadata and album art. Because there are no DRM locks, you can play them in any Android player or back them up to your computer.

How do synchronized lyrics work?

In the native Android app, MIYORI queries LRCLIB for word-for-word synchronized timestamps and reads embedded USLT/ID3 tags directly from your local files. In this website demo, the lyrics and music are original procedural compositions by MIYORI Labs.

Is MIYORI open source?

Yes. MIYORI is maintained transparently on GitHub at github.com/bk5859200-blip/MIYORI with zero proprietary ad trackers or analytics SDKs.
